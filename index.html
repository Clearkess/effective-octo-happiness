FAQ

Common onboarding questions

How do I create an account

Create an account, complete your profile details, and continue to the KYC screen.

How does identity verification work

You work through five steps: your profile details, a photo of your ID, proof of address, a selfie, and a final review.

How do deposits and withdrawals appear

Your dashboard shows every deposit, buy and withdrawal, and gives you your deposit address and a withdrawal form.

Can I connect a wallet

Yes. You can connect a browser wallet such as MetaMask, and you can use your account without one if you prefer.
