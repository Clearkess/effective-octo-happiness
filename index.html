Open the deposit page from your dashboard to get the deposit address for the asset and network you want to use, then send funds from your own wallet or exchange. If a deposit does not appear on your account, contact support with the transaction hash.

BlockHarborMarkets is a self-custody account and portfolio platform. We do not hold client funds, execute trades on your behalf, or provide investment advice. For questions about our regulatory status, contact support.

Submit a withdrawal request from your dashboard with the amount and the destination address. Requests are recorded against your account as pending and are reviewed before they are processed.

Fee information is not currently published on this site. Contact support for the current schedule before you transact.
